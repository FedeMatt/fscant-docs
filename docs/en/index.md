# F-SCANT

Instruction manual for the computer software that works with your F-SCAN.

**Product:** F-SCANT  
**Release year:** 2026  
**Supported computers:** Mac and Windows  
**Manufacturer:** TB-ELECTRONICS GmbH

## Introduction

F-SCANT is a program you install on your computer, enhancing what you can do with an F-SCAN device. It allows you to:

- **DIRP:** start a new DIRP or import one from your F-SCAN device
- **Analysis:** analyze DIRPs, match pathogens, and work with programs
- **Manage users:** manage your list of users, their DIRPs and programs
- **Settings:** manage date & time format and language, import data from older software

Words in **bold** are the exact names of buttons and labels on the screen.

Most of F-SCANT works on the computer alone but for few steps your F-SCAN device must be plugged in. Those steps are marked with this tag:

**Needs F-SCAN**

This manual describes F-SCANT. For instructions on the F-SCAN itself, please use the instruction manual that came with your device.
